01 / BOTANICAL OUTLIER

The Dragon Lily Challenge

Dracunculus vulgaris (Dragon Lily) possesses a deep maroon spathe and tall black spadix spike, completely violating common 6-petal trumpet lily morphology. Traditional models fail on it. By curating verified research-grade observations directly into our dataset, MobileNetV2 identifies Dragon Lilies as authentic lilies with >90% confidence.

02 / EMPIRICAL DISCOVERY

The Scraper Contamination Lesson

Initial web-scraped data contained ~32% noise (maps, herbarium sheets, medals, insects), capping CNN accuracy at 61.0%. Rebuilding with 15,276 pure research-grade images yielded a +18.6% accuracy surge on MobileNetV2 (85.11%), proving that Data Quality > Data Quantity.

03 / OPEN-SET FORMULATION

Open-Set Non-Flower Rejection

Standard closed-world binary classifiers force softmax outputs to sum to 100% on arbitrary inputs (e.g., cars or animals). We formulated a 3-class gating engine: if non-flower confidence exceeds 40%, outputs strictly collapse to 0.0% Lily and 0.0% Other Flowers.
